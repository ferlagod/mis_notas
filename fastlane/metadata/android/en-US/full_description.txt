<b>Mis Notas</b> is a free and open-source note-taking app for Android. It combines the clean look of the best-known notes apps with folders, Markdown and <b>end-to-end encrypted</b> sync with your own Nextcloud server.

<b>No ads, no tracking</b>
No ads, no telemetry, no mandatory accounts and no proprietary libraries. It works offline and without an account: sync is optional. 100% free software under the GNU GPLv3.

<b>Your cloud, your rules</b>
Notes are encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM before being uploaded to your Nextcloud (WebDAV). The server only stores unreadable data. If a note is edited on two devices at once, both versions are kept.

<b>Main features:</b>
• Nested folders with colors, pinned notes and a trash you can restore from.
• Markdown: headings, bold, italic, strikethrough, lists, checklists you tick with a tap, tables, quotes, code and links.
• Editor with undo and redo, lists that continue automatically and a formatting bar.
• Image attachments with a zoomable viewer.
• Multi-select to pin, color, move or delete several notes at once, with "Undo".
• Instant search that ignores accents and highlights matches.
• Grid or list view, sorted by date or title.
• Share notes and create notes from any app.
• Markdown backup (ZIP with folders and images) compatible with Obsidian, Joplin and other apps.
• App lock with fingerprint, face or PIN.
• Designed for foldables and tablets, with the list and the note side by side.
• Light, dark or pure black AMOLED theme.
• Available in English, Spanish, French, German and Italian.
